Privacy questions.

01

Can Nova work without the internet?

Yes. Local dictation and rewriting can run directly on a compatible Windows PC.

02

What leaves my PC when I enable Turbo?

The current audio request is sent over TLS for speech transcription. Nova retains neither the audio nor the returned text; only a usage counter is kept.

03

Does Nova upload screenshots by default?

No. Accessibility and local OCR are used first. Cloud vision is an optional, permission-based last resort, and the image is not stored by Nova.

04

Is private Organization infrastructure available automatically?

No single topology is assumed. Private deployment options are evaluated and confirmed during a Campus or Business pilot.
