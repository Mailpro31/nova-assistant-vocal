Questions

Everything we get asked.

Does Nova work offline?

Yes. Private Intelligence listens, transcribes and rewrites entirely on your computer — no connection, no account. Only Turbo, Nova Ultra's online option, uses the internet; and without a connection, Nova falls back to Private Intelligence on its own.

What machine do I need?

A PC running Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and a microphone — a laptop's built-in mic is fine. Nova measures your machine and picks the right power profile by itself: 8 GB of RAM is enough, more unlocks the higher-quality profiles.

Are my dictations private?

By design. With Private Intelligence, the audio, the text and your vocabulary never leave your computer — we cannot read them. No account, no tracking cookies. Details in the privacy policy.

How does Nova read the on-screen context?

Context reading (Nova Ultra) lets Nova understand the active window — who you're replying to, about what — to write a fitting reply. It runs locally by default: Windows accessibility, then local text recognition as a fallback. A cloud option, Ultra-only and off by default, is used only at your request and never stores the image. Learn more.

Which languages are supported?

More than 90 languages, detected automatically — dictate in French, switch to English mid-thought if you like. Rewriting follows the language you speak.

Can I cancel? What about refunds?

Cancel anytime, no questions asked, from the billing portal — Nova stays active until the end of the period. Nova gives you 14 days of Pro on install, no card. Refund terms are detailed in the terms of sale.

On how many computers?

Each license activates Nova on two computers. A device unused for 30 days frees its seat automatically — handy when you switch machines.
