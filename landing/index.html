Questions.

01

What does Nova do?

Nova lets you speak instead of type in your existing Windows applications. It can dictate, rewrite, reply, summarize, translate and adapt the result to the context.

02

Does Nova retain my audio or text?

In Private Intelligence mode, your content stays on your PC. Optional Turbo processes audio transiently, returns the text and keeps neither the audio nor the result. Only a usage counter is retained.

03

How does context awareness work?

Nova uses local accessibility and OCR first. Optional cloud vision is a last-resort feature that requires your permission; the active-window image is processed to produce context and is not stored by Nova.

04

Which Nova is right for me?

Personal is for individual work. Campus adds controlled deployment for education. Business brings multi-seat workflows and organization-level control.

05

Can Nova work offline?

Yes. Local dictation and rewriting can run directly on your computer. Online features remain optional.
