Questions

Clear answers.

Does Nova work without an internet connection?+

Yes. Local dictation and rewriting can run directly on your computer. Cloud features such as Turbo require a connection and are opt-in.

What computer do I need?+

A 64-bit Windows 10 or 11 PC, a microphone and at least 8 GB of RAM.

Does Nova keep my dictations?+

In Local mode, audio, text, vocabulary and history stay on your computer. When cloud features are enabled, content is sent for the requested processing and Nova does not build a content archive from it.

Are Campus and Business available now?+

Nova is opening scoped Campus and Business pilots. Deployment, private runtime and data boundaries are validated with each organization before rollout.

Are the application brands shown Nova partners?+

No. They illustrate where Nova can be used. They do not imply partnership, endorsement or customer status.
