What does a campus pilot include?

We define a focused group, priority workflows, privacy requirements and success criteria before deployment.

02

Can Nova run locally?

Yes. Local dictation and rewriting can run directly on compatible Windows PCs.

03

Does Nova use student work to train models?

Nova does not retain Turbo audio or text for training. Local content remains on the device.
