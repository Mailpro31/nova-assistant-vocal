Campus FAQ

Before a pilot.

Is Nova Campus a finished campus-wide platform?+

Nova Campus is currently presented as a scoped pilot program. The pilot is used to validate product fit, architecture and operational requirements before any broader rollout.

Can students use Nova locally?+

Yes. Nova Personal includes Local mode on compatible Windows PCs. The pilot defines when Local or cloud-enabled features are appropriate.

Does Nova currently include SSO or SCIM?+

Those capabilities are not represented as generally available. Identity and administration requirements are captured during the pilot and only published once technically validated.

What does a pilot measure?+

Typical goals include activation, writing-time saved, qualitative usefulness, accessibility, privacy fit and operational readiness.
