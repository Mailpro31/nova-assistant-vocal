Business questions.

01

Where does Nova work?

Nova writes at the active cursor across Windows applications, including email, documents, messaging tools and AI interfaces.

02

Can core workflows remain local?

Yes. Local dictation and rewriting can run directly on the Windows device.

03

What does Turbo retain?

Turbo retains neither audio nor text. It processes the request, returns the transcription and keeps only a usage counter.

04

How do we start?

We define a focused pilot around priority users, workflows, security requirements and measurable outcomes.
