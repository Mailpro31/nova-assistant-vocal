Terms of Sale

Nova is distributed as software for Windows. The features, price and billing period for each paid plan are shown on the pricing page and again at checkout before payment.

Plans and access

  • Free provides the core Nova experience described on the landing page.
  • Nova Pro and Nova Ultra unlock the additional features shown in their respective plan cards.
  • A Nova license may be active on up to two computers, as indicated in the product FAQ.

Changes and cancellation

Any subscription management, cancellation options and renewal information should be reviewed in the payment flow or account area associated with the purchase.

Privacy

Nova is designed around local processing. When Local mode is used, audio, text and vocabulary are intended to remain on the user’s computer. Features that rely on external services may involve additional processing that should be described at the point where those features are enabled.

Payments

Payment information is handled by the payment provider used at checkout. Nova should not require storing full card details on this landing site.

Billing

The pricing page may offer monthly and annual billing. When an annual option advertises “2 months free”, the annual amount shown on the pricing page is the amount presented for that billing period. The final amount and any applicable taxes should always be verified at checkout.

Legal Notice

The production version of this page should include the legally required publisher and business information for the entity operating Nova, together with an appropriate contact address and any registration details that apply.

This is a preview legal hub for the current landing-page branch. It should be reviewed and completed with the final business identity, jurisdiction-specific terms and production contact details before being used as the definitive legal documentation.
