Personal questions.

01

Can Nova work offline?

Yes. Local dictation and rewriting can run directly on your Windows PC.

02

Does Nova work inside my existing apps?

Yes. Nova writes at the active cursor, so you can keep working in the applications you already use.

03

Does Turbo keep my audio or text?

No. Turbo processes audio transiently, returns the transcription and retains neither the audio nor the text. Only a usage counter is stored.

04

What computer do I need?

A 64-bit Windows 10 or 11 PC, a microphone and at least 8 GB of RAM.
