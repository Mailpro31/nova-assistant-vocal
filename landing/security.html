Three clear paths

Know where
the work happens.

The privacy story changes with the mode. Nova explains that difference instead of collapsing every workflow into one absolute promise.

Personal · Local

On your Windows PC.

Local transcription, rewriting, vocabulary and history remain on the device.

  • Available offline
  • Local SQLite history
  • Windows DPAPI for stored secrets
  • No remote server required
Personal · Turbo

Only when enabled.

Content is sent for the requested cloud processing. Nova does not use it to build a content history.

  • Opt-in setting
  • Task-bound processing
  • Local fallback
  • Provider terms can apply
Organization · Scoped

Validated before rollout.

Private runtime, identity and policy requirements are architecture work, not unchecked marketing claims.

  • Data-flow review
  • Infrastructure scoping
  • Pilot validation
  • Documented boundaries

What Nova can defend today

Strong claims.
Specific scope.

The current product supports local transcription, local rewriting options, local history and Windows-protected stored secrets. It also supports opt-in cloud features with local fallback.

  • “Local mode runs on your PC” — supported
  • “Cloud features are opt-in” — supported
  • “Everything always stays on-device” — intentionally not claimed
  • SSO, SCIM and central policies — not claimed as generally available

Privacy FAQ

No fine-print hero.

Does everything always stay on my device?+

No blanket claim is made. Local mode keeps supported processing and history on your PC. Turbo and other cloud-enabled features send content for the requested processing.

Does Nova train on my content?+

Nova does not use dictated content to build a Nova content history or training corpus. Third-party provider terms may apply when their cloud processing is enabled.

Where is my history stored?+

Nova’s application history is stored locally in SQLite on the user’s device.

What is promised for Organization deployments?+

Only the scoped discovery and pilot process is promised here. Dedicated runtime, identity and policy capabilities must be technically validated before rollout.
